Apple HomeView

A full-screen wall display of the lab for a 1088 × 1088 touch screen. The square canvas scales to fit any screen, without scrolling.

Open full screen
How it works

Live data from the modules that are on, refreshed every minute:

  • Home: clock, vCenter, what needs your attention
  • Lab, Clusters, Hosts, VMs, Health: DBH Insights
  • Storage: Datastore Capacity (with runway)
  • GitHub: GitHub Status (an example of an outside source)

Lists longer than one screen become several pages. Auto-cycle shows every page for 20 seconds by default; change the time under Settings. It is on by default and remembered on that screen. Tap the home icon at the top left for the dashboards menu. Arrow keys or a swipe go to the next page.

Bookmark /homeview on the wall screen, or a page such as /homeview#hosts.
